How to use
  1. Connect – press Connect and pick a thermometer running pvvx custom, ATC1441 or ZigbeeTLc (BLE mode) firmware. Devices with stock firmware are flashed on the Flasher page first.
  2. Change settings – the current configuration is read from the device. Edit it and press Send config; the device confirms by sending its new configuration back.
  3. More – the sections below the configuration hold the comfort zone, triggers, sensor calibration, keys & security (name, MAC, PIN, bind key), the clock, the measurement history and a raw command console. Charts of the readings are on the Live data and History pages.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Opera (desktop and Android) over HTTPS. A device accepts one connection at a time: disconnect it in other tabs and apps before connecting here.

Based on pvvx/ATC_MiThermometer TelinkMiFlasher.
