3 Activation & settings

How to use
  1. Connect – press Connect and pick your thermometer. Wake it up (press a button or remove/insert the battery) if it does not show up.
  2. Stock Xiaomi / Qingping firmware – press Do activation first. This registers the device with this browser (as the Mi Home app would) and shows the token and bind key. Devices locked to newer stock firmware need a token from the Xiaomi cloud and Login with token.
  3. Choose a firmware – load the recommended Custom firmware (or Zigbee / original) or a local file, then press Start flashing. Keep the page open and the device near the computer; it reboots when done.
  4. Configure – reconnect after flashing to change advertising format (BTHome, PVVX, ATC, Mi), intervals, display options, calibration and more.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Opera (desktop and Android) over HTTPS. A failed or aborted transfer leaves the current firmware in place; just try again. Flashing third-party firmware is at your own risk.

Based on pvvx/ATC_MiThermometer TelinkMiFlasher and atc1441's original flasher. Firmware is downloaded from the upstream repositories.
