1 Wiring

Recovers or flashes a device without Bluetooth: the adapter's TX line drives the chip's SWire debug pin (SWS). Works with most USB-UART adapters (CH340, CP2102, FT232, PL2303) running at 3.3 V.

  1. GND → −Vbat (battery minus)
  2. +3.3V → +Vbat (remove the battery)
  3. TX → SWS pad
  4. optional: RTS → RESET for an automatic reset

The pads for each device are shown on the firmware project page. Without RTS → RESET, set an activation time longer than the device's advertising interval, or power-cycle it during activation.
